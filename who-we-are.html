Who we are

A committed team behind a clear mission.

Pads for Peers works to make menstrual products accessible to students and to build a supportive, open culture around periods in schools.

Our mission

Comfort, access, and dignity at school.

Our mission is to make comfortable and good-quality menstrual hygiene products available and accessible to students who need them.

We also work to cultivate a supportive, open culture around periods in these schools.

A practical partnership

Your school helps create the access point.

Pads for Peers coordinates onboarding, materials, box preparation, and monthly delivery.

School staff provide a designated pickup location, display student information posters, and hand boxes to students when they arrive for pickup.

Start a conversation ↗

There is no cost to the school.

What the school provides

  • A designated pickup location such as the front office or nurse’s office.
  • Space for student information posters.
  • A staff member to hand boxes to students at pickup.

Students enroll privately using a QR code or link and select the products they need.

Common questions

What schools ask us.

Is there a cost to the school?

No. The program is designed to provide support to students without cost to the school.

How much work is involved for staff?

In most cases, staff provide a pickup location and hand boxes to students when they come to collect them.

Do students sign up publicly?

No. Enrollment and pickup are designed to be private and discreet.

Do we need to manage inventory?

The program is designed to keep logistics simple for schools and minimize school-side overhead.

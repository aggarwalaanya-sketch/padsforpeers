Understanding the barriers to menstrual care.

Period poverty includes difficulty accessing menstrual products and the stigma that can make it harder to seek support.

Care.Choice.Dignity.

The issue

Access affects everyday life.

When people cannot get the products they need, managing a period at school or work becomes harder.

Pads for Peers responds with school-based product access and educational resources that support more informed, open conversation.

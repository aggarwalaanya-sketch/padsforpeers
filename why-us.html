Why Pads for Peers

Access works best when students can use it.

Products alone are not enough if students cannot get them when they need them. Our school-based model centers access, choice, privacy, and continuity.

Care.Choice.Dignity.

The need

No student should have to manage without what they need.

The school day is easier when menstrual care is dependable and discreet.

Without an accessible option, students may have to ask staff, find an informal solution, or go without products. Pads for Peers works through schools to make support easier to find and use.

Designed around students

Four principles guide the program.

01

Access

Support is offered through a place students already know.

02

Choice

Students select products that fit their individual needs.

03

Privacy

Discreet enrollment and pickup help protect confidentiality.

04

Continuity

Monthly boxes make support consistent and predictable.

Beyond products

Open conversation matters, too.
